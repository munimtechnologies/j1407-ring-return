# Scoop check, 2026-11-26 (at grading, ~08:00 UTC)

Question: has anyone published a post-2018 photometric test of J1407b's bound-orbit periods, or a
second eclipse of J1407 (V1400 Cen)?

1. arXiv API, all:J1407 OR all:"V1400 Cen", newest first (16 results): the J1407-star papers are
   Klaassen & Kenworthy 2025 (arXiv:2512.13254, ALMA Band 7 non-detection, no photometry), van Dam &
   Kenworthy 2024 (arXiv:2404.07378, light-curve modelling of 2007 only), Barmentloo, Dik et al. 2021
   (arXiv:2106.15902, search for small transiting companions; no period constraint on the ring
   companion), Kenworthy et al. 2020 (arXiv:1912.03314). None tests the ring companion's period with
   post-2018 data.
2. Web search "J1407b ring system second eclipse orbital period constraint 2025 2026": only
   2014-2018 papers (Kenworthy 2015, Kenworthy & Mamajek 2015, Rieder & Kenworthy 2016, Mentel 2018).
3. Earlier searches of 2026-11-24 (notes/scoop-check-2026-11-24.md): ASAS-SN/TESS "no eclipse",
   "V1400 Cen" 2025/2026 photometry: nothing.

Closest prior work: Mentel et al. 2018 (arXiv:1810.05171), photographic plates and photometry to
2018: period 11-18 yr or > 22 yr, next eclipse from spring 2018, most plausibly 2021-2024.
New here: the first test of the 2018-2025 predicted returns with ASAS-SN g-band and TESS under
pre-registered rules: 77% of 11-18 yr and 85% of 14-17 yr excluded; the surviving islands named.
